The destructor of the base \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_thin_var_dep_interface}{Thin\+Var\+Dep\+Interface}} class has nothing to do. However, it is important to remark that the destructor of any derived class {\itshape is} (unlike that of \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_variable}{Variable}}) assumed to scan through the list of \char`\"{}active Variable\char`\"{} of this \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_thin_var_dep_interface}{Thin\+Var\+Dep\+Interface}} and remove the itself from then. The idea is that \begin{DoxyVerb}*Variable are constructed before the "stuff" they are active
 in and destructed after them*
\end{DoxyVerb}


and hence, before that a \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_thin_var_dep_interface}{Thin\+Var\+Dep\+Interface}} can be destructed, it should in principle clear up the data structure in its \char`\"{}active\char`\"{} \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_variable}{Variable}}, to allow them to safely destruct themselves without making any reference to no longer existing object.

However, this means that a nontrivial amount of work may be done pointlessly when destructing a \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_block}{Block}}, in that the \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_thin_var_dep_interface}{Thin\+Var\+Dep\+Interface}} will have to update the data structures linking \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_variable}{Variable}} to them right before the \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_variable}{Variable}} themselves are destructed. This is why the clean() method is provided to do the \char`\"{}guts of destructor\char`\"{} {\itshape without} the removing from the \mbox{\hyperlink{class_s_m_spp__di__unipi__it_1_1_variable}{Variable}}, which helps in avoiding useless work.
